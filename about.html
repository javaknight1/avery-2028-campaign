About the Candidate

Meet Rob Avery

Builder. Optimist. Recovering tab-hoarder. Running for President because the country deserves a government that actually ships.

Rob Avery

Rob Avery spent his career shipping things that had to actually work. Not slide decks, not press releases — working systems, with deadlines, where "good enough in theory" gets you fired. Somewhere along the way he got tired of watching the country settle for things that don't work, defended by people who'll never have to use them.

His pitch is almost boring in its simplicity: treat government like a serious engineering problem. Define the goal in plain language. Fund it honestly. Measure it in public. And when something isn't working, say so and fix it — instead of declaring victory and moving on.

"Optimism, with receipts. We can have nice things — if we're willing to build them and count them."

Why he's running

Because the gap between what we can build and what we actually build has never been wider. We can wire a continent, clean a grid, cure diseases, and put a roof over every family — and yet too often we choose paralysis, blame, and decline. Rob thinks that's a choice. He'd like to make a different one.

He's running an unusual campaign: policy drafted in the open, costs shown honestly, and tools — like the Tax Lab on this very site — that let you check the math yourself instead of taking anyone's word for it.

What he believes

Build

The future is something you make, not something you wait for. Bias toward action.

Honesty

Show the costs, the trade-offs, and the scoreboard. No 600-page mysteries.

Dignity

A good life — housing, health, a fair shot — should be within reach of anyone who works for it.

Stewardship

Leave the country, the climate, and the institutions stronger than we found them.
