Four pillars

⚡

Technology & Innovation

Build the future in America, openly.

  • Gigabit broadband to all 50 states as public infrastructure.
  • A public, audited "AI Bill of Rights" — your data, your call.
  • Open-by-default: publicly funded research is publicly owned.
  • A $1T Future Fund for science, space, and hard moonshots.
💼

Economy & Jobs

Work that pays. A life you can afford.

  • A "build it here" tax credit for small manufacturers.
  • Portable benefits that follow the worker, not the job.
  • Housing: 5 million new homes via a YIMBY federal partnership.
  • Zero-fee small-business starter accounts at the Postal Bank.
🌱

Climate & Energy

Clean power, cheaper bills, real jobs.

  • A clean grid by 2035 — nuclear, solar, wind, and storage.
  • A National Grid Corps to modernize transmission lines.
  • Heat-pump & weatherization rebates for every household.
  • Restore 100M acres and plant a billion native trees.
🎓

Education & Health

Learn for free. Heal without fear.

  • Tuition-free community & technical college, nationwide.
  • Cap insulin and inhalers at $5; cap out-of-pocket care.
  • A teacher pay floor that beats the local cost of living.
  • Universal pre-K and after-school as a public service.

The plan, plainly

No 600-page mystery. Three commitments, written so you can hold us to them.

01

Make it, here.

Re-shore the industries that matter — chips, batteries, medicine, steel — with credits tied to good wages and clean plants. If it's critical, we should be able to build it.

02

Lower the cost of a good life.

Housing, childcare, healthcare, and energy are the four bills that break families. We bring each one down with supply, competition, and hard caps where markets fail.

03

Invest in what's next.

Fund the science, train the workers, and lay the wires. The countries that build the future get to live in it first. We intend to be first.
