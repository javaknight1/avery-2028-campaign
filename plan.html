The First 100 Days

A campaign should tell you exactly what it will do, and when. Here's the plan: what we sign on Day One, the first bills we send to Congress, and a public scoreboard so you can hold us to every line.

Day One

What we sign the first afternoon

Things a president can do alone — no Congress required.

  • Order an ethics reset across the executive branch: no individual-stock trading by senior appointees — diversified index funds or a blind trust only. ↗
  • Direct the Treasury and IRS to go after the tax gap and enforce the law on the wealthiest filers. ↗
  • Kick off GovTracker — the public, live dashboard of federal spending and outcomes. ↗
  • Refocus the EPA on waste, recycling, and reuse, and re-anchor federal climate and clean-energy policy. ↗
  • Stand up a real pandemic-readiness posture and protect the public-health workforce. ↗
  • Direct agencies to publish in plain language — FOIA-by-default, no more 600-page mysteries.
Week 1

The first bills to Congress

Sent immediately — starting with the popular, high-odds reforms.

  • A $15 minimum wage. ↗
  • Universal childcare & paid family leave. ↗
  • Close the carried-interest and like-kind loopholes. ↗
  • Fully fund the VA and end veteran homelessness. ↗
  • The 988 / mental-health & addiction response. ↗
Days 1–100

The big builds, introduced and underway

Longer fights we start immediately and see through.

  • Begin the phased rollout of Universal Healthcare. ↗
  • Launch education reform — pre-K, free trade & community college. ↗
  • Send the full revenue plan — corporate reform, the billionaire tax, the carbon fee. ↗
  • Open the infrastructure partnership with all 50 states. ↗
  • Introduce the constitutional reforms — term limits and Supreme Court fixes — knowing they're long fights. ↗

Day 100: the public scoreboard

At 100 days we publish a signed, public scorecard — every promise above, marked done, in progress, or blocked, with the reason. No spin. You can check our work on GovTracker and the live debt page any day you like.
